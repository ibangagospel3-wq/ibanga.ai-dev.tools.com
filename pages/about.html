ABOUT IBANGA

Learning should lead to making.

IBANGA AI Developers Tool is an original frontend learning environment designed to help new developers move from tutorials to useful projects.

OUR PRINCIPLE

Explain clearly.
Let people experiment.

We believe the fastest way to learn the web is to combine a short explanation with immediate practice. That is why every part of IBANGA connects learning, code, preview, and feedback.

This first version is intentionally frontend-only. It is a friendly foundation for a future API, database, accounts, and production AI service.
